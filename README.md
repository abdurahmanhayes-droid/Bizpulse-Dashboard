BizPulse
Smart Business Analytics for African SMEs
BizPulse is a modern business management and analytics platform designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses in Zambia and across Africa.

The platform helps businesses track:
-Sales
-Inventory
-Cash flow
-Expenses
-Business performance
all from a simple mobile-friendly dashboard.
Built for garages, restaurants, boutiques, car washes, retail stores, salons, and other SMEs, BizPulse replaces notebooks, spreadsheets, and manual tracking with an intelligent digital system designed for African business environments.

Key Features
-Real-time sales tracking
-Inventory management with low-stock alerts
-Cash flow monitoring
-AI-powered business insights
-Business reports and analytics
-Mobile money compatibility
-Multi-user role access
-Mobile-first dashboard

Why BizPulse?
Most African SMEs still operate manually, leading to:
-revenue leakage
-poor financial visibility
-stock losses
-weak decision-making
BizPulse solves this by giving businesses affordable access to tools normally used by large companies.

Business Opportunity
Africa’s SME market is rapidly digitizing, with millions of businesses still lacking proper operational systems. BizPulse is positioned to become a scalable SaaS platform for African SMEs, beginning in Zambia and expanding regionally.

Revenue Model
BizPulse operates on a subscription-based model with affordable monthly pricing tailored for SMEs, creating recurring revenue with high scalability and low operational costs.

Vision
To become the operating system for African small businesses by delivering simple, affordable, AI-powered business management tools built for Africa.
